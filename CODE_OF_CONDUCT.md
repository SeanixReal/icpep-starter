# Community expectations

This is a student learning project. Treat contributors and learners with patience and respect. Critique the work, explain your reasoning, and help people understand how to improve it.

- Welcome questions and first contributions.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or publish personal information.
- Keep feedback relevant and constructive. Avoid mocking a beginner's mistakes.
- Respect device ownership, software licenses, and institutional marks.
- Do not post credentials, private coursework records, or student identifiers.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. Raise conduct concerns privately with a repository maintainer through an established club channel. Do not publish a sensitive incident report in a public issue.
